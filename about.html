A LITTLE ABOUT US

Thoughtful tools.
Real people behind them.

AnyBizHub is the digital product home of Soance Innovations LLP. We build software to help people organise their work and spend more time doing what matters.

WHY ANYBIZHUB EXISTS

Business is personal.
Your tools should get that.

A useful app should make it easier to prepare, remember and follow through — without getting in the way of the relationship itself.

That is the idea behind this product home. AnyBizHub brings together our mobile-app information, clear business details and a straightforward path to support. Our featured product, My BNI, is built for business-networking members.

“Less time finding the details.
More time making the connection.”

OUR APPROACH

Useful, understandable,
and built around people.

01 / PURPOSE

Start with a real task.

Organising a directory. Preparing a meeting. Remembering an important detail. That’s where useful software begins.

02 / CLARITY

Make the details accessible.

Product information, the identity of the provider, and a direct way to ask a question should be easy to find.

03 / RESPONSIBILITY

Respect the relationship.

Business information belongs in the right hands. Use member data responsibly, with permission and a clear purpose.
