CAKE BUILDER · PRIVACY

Cake Builder privacy notice

Effective: 20 October 2026. Cake Builder (com.soance.cakebuilder) is provided by Soance Innovations LLP. This notice covers the Android and iOS apps and the cake studio at cakebuilder.soance.com, including a bakery's branded experience. The notices for Quotation Builder, FitKadam and My BNI on this page apply to those separate products.

For Cake Builder support or privacy requests, email hello@anybizhub.com or visit Cake Builder support.

Information we process and why

You can browse the public menu without an account. Registering provides your name and email address; your password is stored as a secure hash. We use account identifiers and sign-in records to authenticate you, manage your requests and recover your account.

An order request contains the customer name, phone number, preferred pickup date and time, cake specifications, both cake-message lines when filled, customer notes and an optional generated cake preview. Saved requests also contain price estimates, bakery quotes, status, timestamps and bakery preparation notes. The bakery uses these details to review, prepare and respond to the request. A cake preview is generated from your design, not a photo taken from your camera or photo library.

We use technical request details, security checks and operational logs to deliver the service, investigate failures and limit abuse. If you contact support, we receive your message, contact details and any attachments you choose to send, and use them to respond. Please do not send passwords or unnecessary personal information.

Public cake and order links

Sharing is optional. A public cake link exposes the cake design; a public order link exposes cake specifications, estimates, status, any bakery quote and creation/update information. Visibility controls let you choose whether the available name, cake message, customer notes and pickup/delivery schedule fields appear. The phone-number field and private bakery preparation notes are excluded from public order responses. Avoid putting private contact information into messages or notes you choose to publish.

Hiding a field changes public visibility; it does not remove the complete design, order or preview stored for your account and the bakery. Hiding a cake message suppresses both message lines and the public preview image, because lettering may be visible in that image.

Anyone with a public link can view or forward it, and can customise a copy of the design. Messaging and social services may fetch and cache public link previews, which can include a visible cake message or a name you enabled. You can change visibility, revoke an order link, or delete its order. Cake links have no automatic expiry; account deletion removes your cake and order links. Changes cannot recall copies or previews independently saved by other people or services.

Device storage, sessions and permissions

The native app stores favourites, saved design names and details, cake messages, timestamps, preview PNGs and preferences in app-private device storage. These local designs are not account-synced unless you submit or share them. Sign-in tokens and cached account identity are kept in protected device storage. The website and embedded studio also use browser storage for a cake draft and cookies for sign-in.

Local drafts can remain after signing out. In-app account deletion clears the native favourites and saved-design collection on that device, but does not erase the studio's browser draft or copies on other devices. Remove saved designs in the app, and clear the relevant browser or app website data to remove remaining local drafts. Uninstalling the app alone does not delete your server account.

Server sign-in tokens are stored as hashes. Sessions expire after 30 days; one-use native-app-to-studio sign-in tickets expire after 60 seconds. Session expiry is not a promise that every historical security record is immediately erased. Requests use HTTPS. The release has no advertising or analytics SDK and does not request camera, microphone, contacts or precise-location permission. We do not sell your personal information.

Service providers

Cloudflare hosts the service, database and private preview storage and processes network/device information for delivery, Turnstile abuse prevention and operational logging. We store hashed rate-limit identifiers for security. When you request password recovery, Resend receives your account email address and a message containing a short-lived reset link for transactional email delivery, not your account password.

The web studio loads fonts from Google Fonts, including when opened inside the app; Google receives the associated network requests. Providers may process information outside your country. External email, browser, app-store and sharing services you choose operate under their own privacy practices.

Retention, deletion and your choices

Account information, orders and shared cake records remain in the active service until removed. You can delete individual orders in My orders. For account deletion, use Account → Delete account in the app or the Cake Builder account deletion page. After password confirmation, deletion removes your active account, sign-in sessions, reset tokens, owned order/share records, their attached previews and public links. Deletion is permanent. Security identifiers, operational logs, backups and independently retained copies are separate from those active account records; this notice does not promise immediate erasure of every historical copy.

You can request access, correction or deletion of information you have supplied to us by emailing hello@anybizhub.com. Identify Cake Builder and the email used for your account; do not email your password. We may need to verify that the request is yours.

Physical orders and notice updates

Cake Builder sends requests for physical bakery products. No payment or card details are taken in the app. The bakery must confirm availability, the final design, price and pickup arrangements. Account or order deletion does not cancel arrangements already confirmed outside the service; contact the bakery separately.

Only include information necessary for your cake request, including when a cake is for a child. Contact us if you believe personal information has been provided improperly. We will update this notice and its effective date when Cake Builder's data practices change.

QUOTATION BUILDER · PRIVACY

Quotation Builder privacy notice

Effective: 13 October 2026. Quotation Builder (com.soance.quotationbuilder) is provided by Soance Innovations LLP. This notice applies to Quotation Builder on iOS and Android. The notices for other products on this page apply separately. For support and privacy questions, contact hello@anybizhub.com.

Information on your device

The app stores the information you enter on your device: company details, optional logo and header/footer artwork, product and service descriptions and prices, customer contact details, quotation contents, settings and templates. Drawn or uploaded signature images, entered signer names and roles, recorded signing times, public certificate details and signed PDFs are stored with the quotation when you use signing features. This information is used to organise your workspace, fill quotation details from saved products, and generate or retain PDFs and email drafts.

Quotation Builder does not require an account and does not send your workspace to our servers. This version includes no advertising, analytics, tracking or cloud-sync service. We do not collect or sell your quotation, product or customer data through the app.

Files, sharing and email

You choose logo, header, footer or signature images, JSON files and signed PDFs through the device file picker. Exported PDFs and JSON backups contain the information you selected or stored. A quotation signing copy contains the selected quotation, its letterhead and any recorded signatures or archived signed PDF; it does not include unrelated quotations, customer lists or catalog items. When you choose a share target, printer or email application, the selected content is passed to that service. Email drafts are not sent by Quotation Builder; sending is controlled by your email application. Review recipients and attachments before sharing. The privacy practices of services you choose apply to copies they receive.

Certificate signing

When you select a compatible PFX or P12 certificate, the certificate file, private-key information and password are used temporarily in memory on your device to sign the PDF. The app does not save those signing credentials in its workspace, backups or signing copies, and does not send them to our servers. Your original certificate file remains in the location you selected. Signed PDFs contain public certificate and signature information that can be seen by recipients. If you use a separate DSC tool or signing provider, its own privacy practices apply. Do not send certificate files, private keys or passwords to another party or to support.

Retention, deletion and security

Saved workspace information remains on the device until you edit or remove it, replace it by importing a backup, or remove the app's local data through your operating system. We cannot remotely access or delete this local workspace. Exported files, sent emails and copies held by other applications or device backup services must be managed separately. Use device access controls and keep exported files in a location appropriate for their contents. PDF and JSON exports are not password encrypted by this app.

Support and changes

If you contact support, we receive the message, your contact details and any attachments you choose to send, and use them to respond to your request. Do not send passwords or unnecessary customer information. You can ask about access, correction or deletion of information you have supplied to support using the contact above. The app is intended for business use and is not directed to children. We update this notice when the app's data practices change.

FITKADAM · PRIVACY AND ACCOUNT DELETION

FitKadam privacy notice

Effective: 10 October 2026. FitKadam is provided by Soance Innovations LLP. This section covers FitKadam; the My BNI draft below applies to a separate product. For FitKadam support and privacy requests, contact hello@anybizhub.com.

Information used by FitKadam

When you create an account, we process your email, account identifier and profile information. Information you choose to enter can include your name, age, body measurements, goals, fitness preferences and health considerations; workouts, activities and GPS routes; food, hydration, steps, sleep, mood and recovery entries; journal notes, coach messages and memories; meal and progress photos; and feedback. Purchase references and entitlement status support optional Plus access. Notification tokens support optional notifications.

Account data is stored using Supabase. Account features require sign-in; guest previews can keep data on your device. Member activity and health entries can be saved locally while cloud sync is pending. Data remains associated with your account until you remove it or delete the account.

Optional permissions and AI

Camera access is used when you take a meal or progress photo. Location access is used for optional foreground route tracking, which pauses when you leave the app. On supported Android devices, Health Connect optionally imports recent step and sleep totals when you choose to connect or sync; FitKadam does not write Health Connect records. The current iOS build uses manual steps and sleep entries and does not request Apple Health access. You can decline or revoke permissions and use manual entries.

OpenAI is the configured provider for FitKadam's optional AI coaching, personalized plans and meal-photo estimates. Selected messages, conversation context, profile preferences and goals are sent through our backend for these features; meal estimation sends the selected meal photo. Progress photos are not sent for AI analysis. AI requests use the provider's API with response storage disabled. This does not eliminate separate provider security or abuse-monitoring retention. AI estimates and wellness advice are not medical diagnosis or treatment.

Other recipients and security

Firebase Messaging supports notifications. Apple and Google process store payments; FitKadam receives purchase and entitlement references, not your payment-card details. Route maps request tiles from OpenStreetMap infrastructure, which receives the network address and requested map area. Speech input and spoken replies can use your device's selected platform speech services. Internet requests use encrypted transport. Providers may process information outside your country.

Delete your FitKadam account or request data deletion

Request FitKadam account or data deletion

In the app, sign in, open Settings, choose Delete account, and confirm. Successful deletion removes the account, account-owned records and uploaded meal/progress photos, and clears that account's tracking cache on the requesting device. Archived coach messages remain stored when you only clear the chat; they are excluded from future coaching context and removed with account deletion. Disconnecting Health Connect does not remove existing FitKadam logs.

You can request deletion without installing or signing in to the app by emailing hello@anybizhub.com. Include your registered email and say whether you want the entire account or specific records deleted. Never send a password or verification code. We may verify ownership before taking action.

Active account data is removed when account deletion succeeds. Exported files, caches on other devices, app-store billing records, support correspondence and provider backups or security logs may require separate handling. Any records retained for legal, billing, fraud-prevention or security obligations remain only for their applicable purpose and retention period. In our response to an email request, we explain its completion timeframe and any retained categories and applicable periods. Provider retention is governed by the service and its configured controls; response storage being disabled is not a promise of zero retention.

Account deletion does not cancel an Apple or Google subscription. Cancel renewal through the store separately. You can also request access or correction, or withdraw optional permissions. FitKadam is intended for adults aged 18 and over. We update this notice when the app's processing changes.

1. Scope and responsible organisation

AnyBizHub, registered at , is responsible for this website. Contact hello@anybizhub.com about privacy matters.

This notice distinguishes information processed by the AnyBizHub website from data used by the My BNI app. Until this draft is approved and published as an effective policy, the app-specific notice currently linked from the stores remains available on the existing My BNI privacy page (opens in a new tab).

2. Information on this website

The delivered website has no account registration, checkout, advertising pixels, analytics scripts, cookies or browser-storage features. Its navigation and policy pages can be used without supplying personal information. Hosting infrastructure may receive technical request details such as IP address, requested URL, browser information and time of access to deliver and protect the site.

The contact and deletion forms are email composers. Values remain in the current browser page until an pre-filled email is opened. The site does not submit them to a server or save them in browser storage. When you choose to open an pre-filled email, the fields are passed to your email application; nothing is sent until you send the email there.

If you send us a message, we receive its contents and your email address, together with any information or attachments you choose to include. Please avoid sending passwords, OTPs, card details or unnecessary sensitive information.

3. Information used by My BNI

Depending on the features used, the app may handle account identifiers and contact details; chapter and member information; selected roster PDFs and images; your notes, presentations and One-to-One plans; and purchase or subscription references. Some information is kept on the device, while enabled sync features use cloud services.

Optional microphone, speech, photo or file access is used when you activate the related feature and grant permission. Optional AI generation sends selected content to the provider you choose. Supabase may process cloud account and roster data. Apple or Google processes store payments. The current app notice gives the app-specific detail.

5. Service providers and external links

Information may be handled by service providers necessary for hosting, support, app operations and transactions. We may also disclose information when required by lawful process, needed to protect rights or safety, or specifically directed by you. We do not sell personal information.

App-store, payment and support links take you to separately operated services. Their privacy practices apply to information you provide there. This site does not load third-party social widgets, embedded maps or tracking integrations.

Where a future direct checkout uses Razorpay, the payment provider will process the payment information submitted to it. We would receive transaction references and status needed for fulfilment and support, rather than your full card number, CVV, UPI PIN or OTP. The current site has no payment-processing integration.

6. Storage, security and international processing

We use appropriate organisational and technical safeguards for information within our control, but no system can promise absolute security. App and infrastructure service providers may process information in locations other than your own country; applicable contractual and legal safeguards must be followed.

Device copies, cloud records, email correspondence and payment-provider records are separate. Deleting an app from a device does not necessarily remove cloud data or cancel a subscription. Hosting, retention settings and optional providers should be reviewed whenever the service changes.

7. Retention and deletion

We retain information only for as long as it is reasonably needed for the purpose for which it was collected, account administration, support, security, or applicable legal obligations. When no longer required, information should be deleted or de-identified. Backups may not be immediately overwritten.

For verified account or cloud-data deletion requests, our proposed completion target is 30 calendar days. If limited records must be retained for tax, accounting, dispute, fraud-prevention or legal reasons, we will explain the relevant category and basis where permitted. A request does not require us to erase another user’s independently held records.

8. Your choices and privacy requests

You may ask for access to, correction of, or deletion of information associated with you, and withdraw consent for optional processing where applicable. Some requests may need proportionate identity verification. We will explain any lawful restriction or effect on the requested service.

Use the account and data deletion page or email hello@anybizhub.com. Device permissions can be changed in system settings. Subscription management remains a separate store action.

For a privacy grievance, use the subject “Privacy grievance — AnyBizHub”. Our proposed acknowledgement target is 2 business days. If not resolved, reply to the same thread for escalation to the designated partner responsible for customer support. Any legally available complaint rights remain unaffected.

9. Children and sensitive information

The product is intended for adult business-networking members and is not directed to children. Do not create a minor’s account or upload a child’s personal information through this site. If you believe we have received such information without an appropriate basis, contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate action.

Only provide information necessary for the task. Do not use member notes, uploads or AI prompts to store or disclose other people’s sensitive information without a lawful basis and appropriate permission.

10. Changes to this notice

We will update the effective date when this notice changes, and provide additional notice of material changes where appropriate. New analytics, hosting arrangements, AI features, payment channels or forms require a corresponding review of the privacy notice and app-store disclosures.

This draft does not claim regulatory certification or guarantee that every implementation meets all privacy obligations. It must match the live website and actual app practices before it is adopted.
