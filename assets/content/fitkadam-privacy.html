FITKADAM · PRIVACY AND ACCOUNT DELETION

FitKadam privacy notice

Effective: 10 October 2026. FitKadam is provided by Soance Innovations LLP. This section covers FitKadam; the My BNI draft below applies to a separate product. For FitKadam support and privacy requests, contact hello@anybizhub.com.

Information used by FitKadam

When you create an account, we process your email, account identifier and profile information. Information you choose to enter can include your name, age, body measurements, goals, fitness preferences and health considerations; workouts, activities and GPS routes; food, hydration, steps, sleep, mood and recovery entries; journal notes, coach messages and memories; meal and progress photos; and feedback. Purchase references and entitlement status support optional Plus access. Notification tokens support optional notifications.

Account data is stored using Supabase. Account features require sign-in; guest previews can keep data on your device. Member activity and health entries can be saved locally while cloud sync is pending. Data remains associated with your account until you remove it or delete the account.

Optional permissions and AI

Camera access is used when you take a meal or progress photo. Location access is used for optional foreground route tracking, which pauses when you leave the app. On supported Android devices, Health Connect optionally imports recent step and sleep totals when you choose to connect or sync; FitKadam does not write Health Connect records. The current iOS build uses manual steps and sleep entries and does not request Apple Health access. You can decline or revoke permissions and use manual entries.

OpenAI is the configured provider for FitKadam's optional AI coaching, personalized plans and meal-photo estimates. Selected messages, conversation context, profile preferences and goals are sent through our backend for these features; meal estimation sends the selected meal photo. Progress photos are not sent for AI analysis. AI requests use the provider's API with response storage disabled. This does not eliminate separate provider security or abuse-monitoring retention. AI estimates and wellness advice are not medical diagnosis or treatment.

Other recipients and security

Firebase Messaging supports notifications. Apple and Google process store payments; FitKadam receives purchase and entitlement references, not your payment-card details. Route maps request tiles from OpenStreetMap infrastructure, which receives the network address and requested map area. Speech input and spoken replies can use your device's selected platform speech services. Internet requests use encrypted transport. Providers may process information outside your country.

Delete your FitKadam account or request data deletion

Request FitKadam account or data deletion

In the app, sign in, open Settings, choose Delete account, and confirm. Successful deletion removes the account, account-owned records and uploaded meal/progress photos, and clears that account's tracking cache on the requesting device. Archived coach messages remain stored when you only clear the chat; they are excluded from future coaching context and removed with account deletion. Disconnecting Health Connect does not remove existing FitKadam logs.

You can request deletion without installing or signing in to the app by emailing hello@anybizhub.com. Include your registered email and say whether you want the entire account or specific records deleted. Never send a password or verification code. We may verify ownership before taking action.

Active account data is removed when account deletion succeeds. Exported files, caches on other devices, app-store billing records, support correspondence and provider backups or security logs may require separate handling. Any records retained for legal, billing, fraud-prevention or security obligations remain only for their applicable purpose and retention period. In our response to an email request, we explain its completion timeframe and any retained categories and applicable periods. Provider retention is governed by the service and its configured controls; response storage being disabled is not a promise of zero retention.

Account deletion does not cancel an Apple or Google subscription. Cancel renewal through the store separately. You can also request access or correction, or withdraw optional permissions. FitKadam is intended for adults aged 18 and over. We update this notice when the app's processing changes.
