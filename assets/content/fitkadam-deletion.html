Delete your FitKadam account and data

Available from 10 October 2026. You can request deletion even if you have uninstalled FitKadam or cannot sign in. There is no fee to submit a request.

Request deletion by email

  1. Email hello@anybizhub.com with the subject FitKadam account or data deletion request.
  2. Use your registered email if possible and identify the FitKadam account. Say whether you want the entire account deleted or only specified records.
  3. Review and send the message in your email app. Opening the email link does not send a request or automatically delete anything. We may ask for proportionate account-ownership verification; never send passwords or verification codes.

Prepare a FitKadam deletion email

Delete in the app

Sign in, open Settings, select Delete account, and confirm the permanent deletion. Wait for the success message. If the request fails or the service is unavailable, email support using the steps above.

Data covered

Deleting your account removes the account credentials and profile; account-owned workout history, activities and GPS routes; food, hydration, steps, sleep, recovery and check-in entries; goals, plans, measurements, journal records, coach history and memories; notification tokens and account-linked entitlement records; and uploaded meal/progress photos. It also clears that account's tracking cache on the device making the request. You may request deletion of specific records by email while keeping the account.

Timing and records handled separately

In-app deletion removes active account records when the operation succeeds. For email requests, support confirms verification, completion timing and any applicable retention exceptions in the response. Exported files and caches on other devices are not remotely erased by deleting the current device's cache. Apple/Google billing records, support correspondence and provider backups or security logs may remain for applicable legal, billing, dispute, fraud-prevention or security retention periods. We identify retained categories and applicable periods when handling a verified request. OpenAI AI-response storage is disabled for FitKadam requests; separate provider security retention may still apply.

Cancel subscription renewal separately in Apple or Google subscription settings. Deleting an account or uninstalling the app does not cancel billing. Save anything you need before confirming permanent account deletion.
