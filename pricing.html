BEFORE ANY CHARGE

Four things to look for.

What you’re buying

The plan name, included features, access period and any membership requirements should be clear before you confirm.

The total you’ll pay

Review the currency, final payable amount, applicable taxes and whether a price changes after a trial or promotion.

How renewal works

Check whether the plan renews automatically, when the next charge is due and how to stop future renewal.

Where to get help

Keep your receipt and transaction reference. The payment channel determines which billing and refund process applies.

BILLING QUESTIONS

A little more clarity.

Read the policy for your purchase channel before paying.

Cancellation & refund policy
Can I cancel an auto-renewing subscription?

Yes. Use the store account that made the purchase, before its renewal deadline. The store will display the cancellation result and remaining access period. See the links on our cancellation page.

Does uninstalling the app stop billing?

No. Uninstalling the app or requesting account deletion is not the same as cancelling your store subscription. Cancel renewal separately in the relevant store.

Can I pay through Razorpay here?

Not currently. This website is for product and business information. Any future website checkout requires a separate, live payment integration and clear direct-sale terms.

Does a website purchase work across both stores?

Do not assume that a purchase or subscription transfers between channels or accounts. Confirm cross-platform access with support before buying again.
