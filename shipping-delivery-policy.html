Shipping & digital delivery

How software and digital access are provided — and why there are no shipping charges or physical parcels.

1. Digital products, not physical goods

AnyBizHub features software and digital services supplied by Soance Innovations LLP, including My BNI. We do not sell physical products on this site. There is no courier shipment, physical shipping charge, delivery address requirement, parcel tracking or physical return process.

2. What this website delivers

The current site supplies product and business information and links to the app stores. It does not create accounts, collect payments, issue licence keys or activate subscriptions. Contact forms prepare email drafts; they do not submit requests directly to a server.

3. App-store download and subscription access

Download the app from the linked Apple App Store or Google Play listing. Download speed and availability depend on the store, network connection, device compatibility and region. The store’s purchase confirmation and the app’s account checks govern any paid access.

If a store purchase is not recognised, confirm you are using the correct store and app account, check the purchase status, and use a restore-purchase option if the app offers it. Do not buy again solely because access has not yet appeared; ask support to investigate first.

4. Proposed delivery for future direct purchases

Before any direct purchase is offered, the order must identify the supplied service, supported account, access period and expected activation or delivery time. Instant digital access should normally follow successful payment and required account verification.

Unless the order expressly states a different timeline before payment, our proposed maximum activation target is 24 hours after a confirmed successful payment and completion of any disclosed eligibility verification. Contact us if access remains unavailable beyond that point.

Any separately agreed project or scheduled service must have its own written delivery milestone. An email receipt or a payment confirmation is evidence of a transaction; it is not, on its own, proof that app access has been activated.

5. What the customer needs

Use accurate contact and account information, a compatible supported device, and an internet connection where needed. A networking app may require verification against an eligible chapter-member record. Check any access conditions before purchase; payment does not establish third-party membership.

Do not assume that a purchase made through one store or account transfers to another. Confirm compatibility and access with us before making an additional purchase.

6. Delays and non-delivery

Email info@soance.com with your account email, purchase channel, transaction reference and a short description. Include a redacted screenshot where useful, but never a password, OTP or payment credential. Our proposed acknowledgement target is 2 business days.

If a direct service cannot be supplied within the agreed timeframe, we will explain the issue and offer an appropriate resolution or refund for the undelivered service under the cancellation and refund policy. Store-billed purchases follow the relevant store’s remedies.

7. Availability and changes

App-store distribution can depend on device, region, account and platform restrictions. Service interruptions may occur during maintenance or because of third-party outages. Any material change to a purchased offer will be communicated as required, without removing mandatory consumer rights.

These digital-delivery terms should be updated before adding physical goods or a different type of service to the site.
